- `ProviderSummarySchema = z.object({ id: z.string(), name: z.string(), service: ServiceSchema, area: z.string(), price: PriceSchema, currency: CurrencySchema, rating: z.number().min(0).max(5), slots: z.array(SlotSchema), attributes: z.array(z.string()) })`.

- `BookingStatusSchema = z.enum(['confirmed','cancelled','rescheduled'])`.
- `BookingSchema = z.object({ booking_id: z.string(), user_id: z.string(), provider_id: z.string(), provider_name: z.string(), service: ServiceSchema, slot: SlotSchema, price: PriceSchema, currency: CurrencySchema, status: BookingStatusSchema, created_at: IsoDateTimeSchema, updated_at: IsoDateTimeSchema })`.
- `HoldSchema = z.object({ hold_id: z.string(), provider_id: z.string(), user_id: z.string(), slot: SlotSchema, price: PriceSchema, search_price: PriceSchema, currency: CurrencySchema, expires_at: IsoDateTimeSchema })`.
- `ToolErrorCodeSchema = z.enum(['bad_request','unauthorized','provider_not_found','slot_unavailable','hold_not_found','hold_expired','slot_taken','booking_not_found','already_cancelled','booking_cancelled','slot_conflict','idempotency_mismatch','transient','timeout','search_error','not_found','internal'])`.

- `TOOL_ERROR_HTTP_STATUS: Record<ToolErrorCode, number>` = bad_request 400, unauthorized 401, provider_not_found 404, hold_not_found 404, booking_not_found 404, not_found 404, slot_unavailable 409, slot_taken 409, already_cancelled 409, booking_cancelled 409, slot_conflict 409, idempotency_mismatch 409, hold_expired 410, transient 503, search_error 503, timeout 504, internal 500.
